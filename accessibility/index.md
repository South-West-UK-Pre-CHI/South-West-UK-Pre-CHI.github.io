South West UK Pre-CHI is committed to providing an inclusive environment and we will do our best to accommodate requests for special assistance. This page describes the accessibility features of South West UK Pre-CHI 2027 to help you make an informed decision about whether the conference will be accessible to you. Please contact us at [{{ contact }}](mailto:{{ contact }}) if your question is not answered here, or if the conference arrangements as described are not enough to allow you to attend. We will work with you to the best of our ability to make the conference accessible.
{: .bg-body-tertiary .border-start .border-4 .rounded .p-3 }

Our [Access and Inclusion Plan (AIP)]({{ access_plan }}) provides more detail about what to expect at the event, including directions to rooms with photos. Printed copies will be available at the conference. If you would like the AIP in a different format, please contact us at [{{ contact }}](mailto:{{ contact }}).

<p>You can also download the following FAQs as a PDF: <a href="{{ '/assets/pdfs/Accessibility-FAQ.pdf' | relative_url }}" download>Download Link</a></p>

<details class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">How do I communicate accessibility needs to South West UK Pre-CHI 2027 organizers?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

When you register for the conference, there will be a registration question labeled “Do you have a disability or special need which requires our awareness?”. In your response, please indicate any accessibility needs such as wheelchair access, sign language interpretation, or a guide. The Accessibility Chair will follow up with you to clarify your needs. You can contact them directly at any time by emailing [{{ contact }}](mailto:{{ contact }}).

</div>
</details>

<details class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">Who do I ask if my question is not answered here?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

The University of Bristol provides information about [accessibility at the venue]({{ venue_facilities }}) and [accessible facilities across campus](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/students/support/disability-support/campus/).

If there is specific accessibility information you would like to see here, or if you wish to discuss any conference accessibility requirements, please contact our Accessibility Chair at [{{ contact }}](mailto:{{ contact }}). Someone will respond to you shortly.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">Will sign language interpretation or captioning be available?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

The conference will endeavour to provide a team of British Sign Language interpreters or captioners if requested by any attendees during the early registration period. Please make your request as early as possible. Our Accessibility Chair will follow up with you to discuss your needs in more detail. After 1 March 2027, we will do our best to accommodate requests, though availability of interpreters or captioners may be limited.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">What are the taxi and public transport options for getting to the conference venue?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

The Sloane Robinson Building is next door to Bristol Temple Meads station. We recommend arriving by train and using the eastern exit, signposted for the University, for step-free access to the conference. Head towards the higher platform numbers. The exit faces the Sloane Robinson Building. You can call a taxi or take the [First Bus number 8](https://www.firstbus.co.uk/bristol-bath-and-west/plan-journey/timetables?service=8) from Temple Quarter Campus to explore the city.

The [University entrance and exit](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/stations/bristol-temple-meads/) is open from 05:45 to midnight Monday to Saturday, and from 07:00 to 23:00 on Sundays. Outside these hours, use the station entrance and exit on Station Approach.

[Visit Bristol](https://visitbristol.co.uk/) has general information about the city.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">Can a student volunteer assist me during the conference?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

Student volunteers will be available to assist attendees with disabilities with navigation, meal service, or other accessibility needs. If you will need volunteer assistance at the conference, please indicate this on your conference registration form.

Attendees who require personal care assistance should bring their own assistant. Personal care assistants should also register for the conference so we can provide them with food and drink. Registration for South West UK Pre-CHI is, and always has been, free of charge.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">What is the conference space like?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

The [Sloane Robinson Building]({{ venue_facilities }}) has lifts and an accessible main entrance, meeting spaces, and on-site restaurants. Service animals are welcome without a fee or documentation. On-site parking is available for Blue Badge holders only. Please use your registration form to request a Blue Badge parking space in advance.

If you think you will need an evacuation plan, please contact the Accessibility Chair, Grace Stangroome, at [{{ contact }}](mailto:{{ contact }}).

See the other FAQs on this page for details about wheelchair or power scooter access, arrangements for presenters, restrooms, walking and steps, and facilities for assistance animals.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">Will the conference be accessible by wheelchair or power scooter?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

Yes, the conference will be accessible to wheelchair users. The venue’s main entrance and meeting spaces are accessible to wheelchair users. Lifts serve the guest and meeting rooms. The meeting rooms will have wheelchair spaces by the central aisle. Student volunteers will be available on request to help with the lunch buffet, and there will be space for wheelchairs at the lunch tables.

Presentations will take place at floor level. Poster sessions will have space for a wheelchair to move between the posters.

</div>
</details>

<details id="restroom-facilities" class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">What are the restroom facilities like?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

The conference venue has accessible toilets in single rooms, as well as all-gender toilets. The [University’s guide to the building]({{ venue_facilities }}) describes the toilet layouts on each floor.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">What are the arrangements for presenters with accessibility needs?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

Presentations will take place at floor level. Please contact the Accessibility team at [{{ contact }}](mailto:{{ contact }}) for more information.

If you are a presenter who uses a wheelchair, please contact us as soon as possible so that we can make arrangements. Poster sessions will have space for a wheelchair to move between the posters.

If you would like a student volunteer to operate slides or guide you to and from the presentation area, or if you have other accessibility requests, please contact [{{ contact }}](mailto:{{ contact }}) by 1 April 2027.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">How much walking or standing will be needed?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

The conference is contained within the Sloane Robinson Building. You may need to move between floors for different activities or tracks. We will use the Bill Brown Forum and the Mezzanine Terrace Studio.

- **Bill Brown Forum (0.401).** Enter through the main entrance into the Exchange Hall. With the Source Cafe on your right, turn left into the main corridor. The Bill Brown Forum is the first door on the left after the lift and stairs.
- **Mezzanine Terrace Studio (M.201).** From the Exchange Hall, go past the Source Cafe on your right and through the door to the lift and stairwell. Take the lift or stairs up one level. Go through the single door on your right if you came up the stairs, or on your left if you took the lift. The Terrace Studio is just to the left.

For detailed directions with images, see the [Access and Inclusion Plan]({{ access_plan }}).

The Sloane Robinson Building has several sets of stairs and lifts, and multiple accessible toilets. Ask a student volunteer for directions to the nearest toilets or see [the FAQ on restroom facilities](#restroom-facilities).

You can also view the [Sloane Robinson Building map](https://use.mazemap.com/#v=1&config=UoBCampuses&view_access_token=fcab572c2d2a4e479089433ce060d24b&campusid=873&zlevel=1&center=-2.578478,51.449537&zoom=17.6&sharepoitype=poi&sharepoi=1003264689).

The building is new, and its AccessAble guide is pending. We will check regularly and update this page if it is published.

Please contact [{{ contact }}](mailto:{{ contact }}) as early as possible if you have further questions or special requests.

</div>
</details>

</div>

<details class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">Will there be loud music, strobe lighting, spotlights, or other strong sensory experiences?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

Please avoid flash photography and strong scents. Signs on the doors will flag demonstrations that involve sudden or loud noises, movement, VR, AR, wearables, or flashing lights.

See [the FAQ on the quiet space](#quiet-space) for more information.

Please contact [{{ contact }}](mailto:{{ contact }}) as early as possible if you have further questions or special requests.

<details id="quiet-space" class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">Will there be a designated quiet space at the conference?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

We have allocated a quiet space at the conference. The location is to be confirmed. The room will be clearly marked on the door and signposted around the event. You can also ask a student volunteer for directions. Please take calls elsewhere and be mindful of electronic device use if sharing the room with other attendees.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">What if I have a medical emergency or health problem during the conference?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

In a medical emergency, call 999 for an ambulance. If you require non-urgent medical care, please contact [{{ contact }}](mailto:{{ contact }}) or ask any student volunteer for information about nearby pharmacies.

</div>
</details>

<details class="accessibility-question border rounded mb-2" markdown="block">
<summary class="fw-semibold px-3 py-2">What will online attendance look like?</summary>
<div class="px-3 pb-1" markdown="block">

We recognise that making events hybrid makes them more accessible to disabled members of our community. Some attendees also prefer in-person presentations at conferences.

We encourage presenters to attend in person, and we will host a Teams webinar with all the talks for those who are unable to attend in person. We will also support workshop and other track authors in making their sessions fun and worthwhile for online attendees. Networking is a valuable part of South West UK Pre-CHI, and we will continue to explore ways to support it for online attendees in the coming months.

If you wish to present your work virtually, please state this on your registration form. We will also provide British Sign Language interpretation for online participants if required.

If you have any other questions, please contact [{{ contact }}](mailto:{{ contact }}).

</div>
</details>
